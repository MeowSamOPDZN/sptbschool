The policy

You have 30 days from delivery to return anything for a full refund. Items should be unused and in their original condition.

How it works

  • Contact us with your order number — we'll send a prepaid return label.
  • Drop the parcel at any carrier point.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 business days of arrival.

Exchanges

Need a different size or color? Return the original and place a new order — it's the fastest way, and return shipping is on us.
