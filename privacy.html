Privacy

What we collect, and what we never do with it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address.
  • Order details: items, shipping address, and order history — so we can fulfil and support your orders.
  • Messages you send through the contact form.

What we don't do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never share it with advertisers.
  • Payment details are processed securely and never stored on our servers.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account and data at any time via the contact page.
